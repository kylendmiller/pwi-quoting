# INCOMMAND TOP RUNNER BRIDGE CRANE SYSTEM #57899

## General Specs:
- Runway OAL:	80'
- Bridge Crane Span:	37'
- Rated Capacity:	2 Ton with (1) hoist
- Hook Height:	13'-7"
- System Voltage:	208V/3 phase

## Crane Classification:
> Class C (Moderate Service): This service covers cranes whose service requirements are deemed moderate, handling loads which average 50 percent of the rated capacity with 5 to 10 lifts per hour, averaging 15 feet, with not over 50 percent of the lifts at rated capacity.

## Runway Package
- (8) Square tube columns spaced at 29'-29'-20' span
- 2 Ton capacity WF beam runway system
- Runway system will include lateral and longitudinal tie back bracing
- (4) HD end stops
- Complete ElectroTrack electrification system
- Runways and columns painted gray
- Square tube support columns with gusseted base plates designed for 6" concrete slab
- Needs to be braced to present structure

## (1) 37’ x 2 Ton Capacity Top Runner Motorized Bridge Crane
- Includes 2 ton top running motorized end trucks with 2-speed/VFD: 100FPM max
- (1) Starke - STK Series 2 ton electric chain hoist
-- Variable speed: 2-26FPM
-- 15’ lift
-- 208V/3ph
- (1) Starke Motorized Trolley
-- 2-speed/VFD: 82/14FPM
-- Lug Mount

## Radio Remote Control System
- STARKE Radio Remote Control system
- Capable of 3 Motion/2 Step control   (N/S - E/W - Up/Down)
- Includes (1) spare transmitter

## Starke Control Panel
- Equipped with VFD control for programmable 2-step control and start - stop operation
- Warning strobe light to indicate bridge is in motion

## Collision Avoidance
- Starke Collision Avoidance for bridge crane to bridge crane and bridge crane to end stops
- Increased safety for operators especially when transporting heavy loads
- Simple uni-directional bridge collision avoidance with (2) adjustable events (0-200 ft)
